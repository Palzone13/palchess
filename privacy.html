Account information

If you create an account, we store your username and a securely hashed password, along with your rating, win/loss record, and game history needed to run the site. We use Supabase to host this data. We don't ask for or store your real name, email address, or any other personal details.

Guest play

If you join a game as a guest, we create a temporary identity for your browser so you can play. This isn't linked to any personal information.

Cookies and similar technology

We use small amounts of local browser storage to keep you signed in and to remember preferences like your sound setting. We don't use tracking cookies of our own.

Advertising

This site shows ads served by Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technology to serve ads based on your visits to this and other sites, and may collect information about your device and browsing activity for that purpose.

  • You can see and adjust the data Google uses for ad personalization at myadcenter.google.com.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising generally at optout.aboutads.info (US) or youronlinechoices.eu (EU).
  • Google's own privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy, and how Google uses data from partner sites is explained at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Children
