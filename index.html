        How it works
        • No turns: any piece that is not cooling down can move at any moment.
        • After landing, a piece rests until its cooldown circle empties.
        • Pieces take time to travel. Capture the enemy king to win.
        • No check or castling. Pawns promote to queens.
        • Click or drag. Right-click and drag draws arrows.

        Right-click a piece to select it, then click or right-click any destination square. The piece moves there and a green arrow marks the move. On touch screens, tap a piece, then tap its destination.
